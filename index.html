How long is a Mooch?

You may remember him as press secretary. He was actually Communications Director. The press secretary that week was Sean Spicer, who quit the very morning the Mooch was hired.

Start: Friday, July 21, 2017 at 2:00 PM EDT, when the White House briefing that introduced him to the press corps was set to begin.

End: Monday, July 31, 2017 at 2:45 PM EDT, the earliest timestamp we could find on a story reporting him out, published 18:45 UTC citing the New York Times' scoop. PBS had it up by 3:04 PM.

2017-07-21 18:00:00 UTC 2017-07-31 18:45:00 UTC ─────────────────────── 10 d 0 h 45 m = 866,700 s

Neither moment was stamped to the second by the White House, so the minutes are our best documented estimate. Swap to the round or generous definition above if you prefer.
